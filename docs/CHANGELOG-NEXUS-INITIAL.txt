Version 1.0.0-rc.4 — Initial public beta

• First Nexus beta release, combining eight feature groups with nine independent switches in Unity Mod Manager.
• Includes Chuff Fix, Bogie Visual Smoothing, CCL Smoke Fix, CCL Control Fix, Loco Sound Pitch, HUD Guard, two Skin Manager fixes and DoubleTrack sign clearance.
• Adds separate steam/diesel pitch strength and lower/upper caps, plus adjustable sign clearance.
• Keeps Chuff Fix's rhythmic loops at native exhaust cadence while other eligible sounds retain their pitch settings.
• Includes live cleanup for chuff and bogie features, dependency-aware status messages and an included toggle/restart guide.
